How the preview works

Each paint begins with chart-sampled colors; single-paint wash steps interpolate five chart samples. Multi-paint proportions combine approximate RGB reflectances using a Kubelka–Munk-inspired model. The automatic recipe finder searches digital approximations, not measured pigment spectra. The water slider blends the resulting wash toward your selected paper color.

Important: HEX codes alone cannot describe pigment absorption spectra, granulation, transparency, or staining. The recipe is a starting point for a real paint test, not a guarantee. For precise matching, paint and scan your own calibration swatches.
